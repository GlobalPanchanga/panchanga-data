Global Panchanga

Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

Global Panchanga is provided by Karthik Dinesh. This policy explains data handling in the current app, which provides precomputed Panchanga and festival information for supported cities and downloadable editable calendars. The phone's home-screen label may appear as Panchanga.

Information on your device

The app stores your default city, favorite cities, downloaded city data and publication information on your device so that you can use downloaded Panchanga information offline. These preferences are not uploaded to a developer-operated account or database. There is no app account or sign-in.

You choose a city manually. The app does not request GPS location, contacts, camera or microphone access. A chosen city does not necessarily represent your physical location.

Downloads and hosting

When you select a city, return to the app or request an update, the app may contact GitHub to check the publication and download the selected city's data. Calendar downloads open GitHub-hosted files in your browser. This privacy page is hosted on GitHub Pages.

To deliver these requests, GitHub and its delivery infrastructure receive technical information such as your IP address, request time, requested file or page and client information sent by your device or browser. A city-specific file request identifies the city being downloaded. GitHub processes service information under its own privacy statement, including its retention and international-processing practices. Download requests use HTTPS. We do not operate a server that records your city selections or build profiles of individual users from downloads.

Calendars you edit

The app downloads prepared PowerPoint calendars. Activities or personal information you add afterwards are handled by your chosen editing or storage application, under its privacy settings and policies. The current app does not upload your edited calendars or temple activities to us. Information saved by an earlier test version may remain locally until you clear app storage; the current calendar screen does not send it.

Support messages

If you email globalpanchanga@gmail.com, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond, investigate issues and maintain relevant support records. Email is handled through our email provider, Google. Please avoid sending passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

Advertising, analytics and disclosure

The app contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDK. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising. Hosting and email providers process information as described above. We may disclose support information where legally required or necessary to protect rights and security. App stores, your operating system and external applications may separately process information under their own policies.

Retention and deletion

Local preferences and downloaded data remain until replaced or removed. On Android, use the system's App info storage controls to clear app storage; on iPhone, delete the app to remove its local data. Removing a favorite does not delete its downloaded city pack. Files saved to Downloads or another application must be deleted separately. Device backups may retain or restore app data according to your device settings.

We retain support correspondence only as long as needed to address the request and for legitimate recordkeeping or legal obligations. Email us to request access, correction or deletion of information you sent; we may need to verify the request and retain information where legally required. GitHub and other providers control retention of their own service records. There is no Global Panchanga account to delete.

Children and security

The app does not knowingly solicit personal information from children. If a child has sent us personal information, a parent or guardian can contact us to request deletion. We use HTTPS for hosted downloads and restrict access to support correspondence; no transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Changes and contact

We will update this page and its effective date when data practices change. New features such as GPS or location requests are not part of the current app; any future data handling will be explained before those features are introduced.
